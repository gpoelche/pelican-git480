What is Pelican?

Pelican is a client-side web application that lets you keep track of the fish you have and haven't caught in Stardew Valley. [NOTE: THIS SECTION IS UNDER CONSTRUCTION]

All Fish Details in One Place

Stardew Valley is often known for being a "wiki game." These are games that often feature a breadth of features, secrets and important information that are hard to keep track of due to the sheer scope of the game. Rather than looking for important information in several places or making a spreadsheet to keep track of your fishing progress, Pelican lets you do it all in one place. You can filter for fish based on their various catch conditions, read important information about each fish and track your progress all within the browser.

Made with the Future in Mind

Even with all of the current features in place, there's a long roadmap of feature planned for Pelican. As a Stardew Valley player myself, it's a priority for this to be the best fishing assistant available to all of the valley's anglers. Ranging from new core functions in the tracker to simple quality of life changes, you can expect to see Pelican continue to grow into the future. Below are just a few features that are in development right now:

Track Between Saves

Want to track your progress in your solo world and with your friends? Soon you'll be able to track fishing progression across several saves.

Plan a Fishing Trip

Have a particular set of fish on your mind? Soon you'll be able to make your own list to help you focus on your current priorities.

A Fresh Coat of Paint

Tired of default light and dark mode themes? Soon you'll be able to change Pelican's style to match your vibe as you fish.
